Automatic deep-zoom precision avoids GPU coordinate banding. Standard 64-bit numbers eventually lose pixel detail near 10¹²× magnification; arbitrary-precision methods are needed to go farther. Mouse wheel zoom is centered on the pointer; pinch zoom works on touchscreens.
